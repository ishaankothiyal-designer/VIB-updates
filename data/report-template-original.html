03Finance & transfer

An active entry deserves a closer look.

A lender entry can matter at transfer. Ask for closure documents and verify the current status.

An entry alone does not establish an overdue loan.

A lender entry, also called hypothecation, records a lender’s interest in the vehicle. The entry does not show how much is owed.

What to ask next

Ask for closure documents

“Do you have the lender’s closure documents?”

Verify the current lender entry and the documents needed before transfer. An active entry does not establish an overdue loan.

What this sample does not establish

A report is useful when its limits are visible.

  • Current or past stolen-vehicle status, court and FIR coverage
  • Actual loan balance or repayment status
  • Accident severity, affected parts or current condition
  • Manufacturer recalls, factory equipment or live comparable sales

Check supporting documents and arrange a physical inspection.
